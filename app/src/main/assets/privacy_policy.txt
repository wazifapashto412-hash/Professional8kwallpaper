In regions where consent is required (e.g. EEA, UK), we ask for your choice through Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) and you can change it later in Settings > Ad privacy choices. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Permissions
- Internet / network state: load wallpapers and detect connectivity.

The app does not request storage, camera, microphone, contacts or location permissions.

5. Downloads
Downloaded wallpapers are saved to Pictures/Infinity Wallpapers on your device (Android 10+) or the app's own Pictures folder (Android 8-9). You can delete them at any time with your gallery or file manager.

6. Image sources and credits
